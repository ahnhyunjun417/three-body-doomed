# Three-Body: Doomed Anyway

A B-grade browser game: mash to grow a civilization on a planet with three suns, dehydrate your people when the weather goes insane, and get doomed anyway by a real three-body physics simulation.

**Play:** https://ahnhyunjun417.github.io/three-body-doomed/

Controls: `Space` / tap to advance, `D` to dehydrate.
